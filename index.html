Our company makes every effort and invests significant resources to provide all its customers with equal, respectful, accessible and professional service. In accordance with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998 and the regulations enacted under it, we invest considerable effort and resources to make the accessibility adjustments required so that a person with a disability can receive the services provided to all customers, independently and equally.

Accessibility arrangements

The internet is today the largest resource for freedom of information for all users, and for users with disabilities in particular. Website accessibility

An accessible website allows people with disabilities and older adults to browse with the same level of efficiency and enjoyment as all users. Approximately 20–25 percent of the population experience difficulty using the internet and may benefit from more accessible internet content (Microsoft research, 2003).

Information about site accessibility

  • This site complies with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility Adjustments), 5773-2013.
  • Accessibility adjustments were made according to the Israeli Standard (IS 5568) for web content accessibility at AA level and the international WCAG 2.0 document.
  • Tests were conducted for the highest compatibility with the Chrome browser.
  • The site provides a semantic structure for assistive technologies and supports the standard usage pattern for keyboard operation using arrow keys, Enter and Esc to exit menus and windows.
  • Adapted for display in popular browsers and for use on mobile phones.
  • For an optimal browsing experience with screen reader software, we recommend using the most up-to-date NVDA software.
  • Documents or videos uploaded to the site before October 2021 may not be fully accessible. If you encounter such a document or video, you may contact the company's accessibility coordinator and we will arrange to make the information accessible to you.
  • Providing information in an accessible format – the office offers customers the option to receive information in accessible formats, free of charge, intended for people with disabilities. For inquiries and information regarding accessibility, you may contact the office's accessibility coordinator whose details appear later in this statement.

We strive to ensure that our digital services are accessible to people with disabilities, in the belief that every person is entitled to live with equality, dignity, comfort and independence. To fulfill this commitment, we strive to adhere as closely as possible to the recommendations of the Israeli Standard for web content accessibility and the international WCAG 2.1 document.

How does accessibility work on the site?

An enable accessibility menu is placed on the site. Clicking the menu opens the accessibility buttons. After choosing a topic from the menu, please wait for the page to load. The software works in the popular browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Opera, subject to the manufacturer's terms.

Menu user guide

  • Site adaptation button and site tags for assistive devices and technologies for people with disabilities
  • Enable navigation using keyboard keys between the links on the site
  • Disable blinking and/or moving elements on the screen
  • Enable monochrome black and white mode for color-blind users
  • Sepia (brown tint) button
  • High contrast mode
  • Black-yellow mode
  • Color inversion
  • Highlight all heading tags on the site
  • Highlight all links on the site
  • Display alternative descriptions of all images on hover
  • Display permanent description of images on the site
  • Disable use of readable font
  • Increase font size on the site
  • Decrease font size on the site
  • Enlarge entire display to ~200%
  • Reduce entire display to ~70%
  • Enlarge mouse cursor
  • Enlarge mouse cursor and change its color to black
  • Site reading mode
  • Display the accessibility statement
  • Reset button to disable accessibility
  • Send accessibility feedback
  • Change the language of the toolbar and accessibility statement accordingly

The accessibility toolbar provides 2 types of zoom for your convenience. To enlarge text further you may use the following keyboard functions:

  • Esc – open and close the accessibility toolbar
  • Ctrl + – enlarge text on the site
  • Ctrl - – reduce text on the site
  • Ctrl 0 – return the site to its original size
  • Space – scroll the site down
  • F11 – enlarge screen to full size; pressing again restores it
